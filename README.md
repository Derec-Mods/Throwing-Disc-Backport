# Throwing Disc Backport

> If this project helped you, please consider leaving some stars [on the Github](https://github.com/Derec-Mods)!

A faithful, accurate backport of the upcoming Throwing Disc weapon revealed by Mojang at TwitchCon. Minecraft players are eating good these past two years, and I'm excited to see what comes next.

None of this is live in vanilla yet, but this backport will remain completely faithful to Mojang's official announcements. We'll roll out updates and fixes as soon as new information drops!

Disclaimer: Not vibe coded. Unlike most of the other backports popping up, this wasn't just churned out of Claude and left broken.

*   Clean ricochets off walls and corners (no phasing through blocks or sound spam)
*   Flies flat and straight with zero gravity drop
*   Works in both main hand and offhand
*   Pierces through mobs and returns to your hand
*   Available on Fabric, Forge, and NeoForge

## Current Features

*   Discs for Copper, Iron, Gold, Diamond, and Netherite tiers
*   Crafting recipes for all tiers
*   Bouncing and loyalty return mechanics
*   Smashes decorated pots on hit

## Planned Features

*   Whatever Mojang announces next
